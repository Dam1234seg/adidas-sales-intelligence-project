ADIDAS SALES ANALYSIS REPORT

1. Introduction

This project focuses on analyzing Adidas sales data to understand sales performance across different regions, products, and retailers. The goal is to identify trends, key drivers of revenue, and provide data-driven recommendations for improving sales performance.

2. Objective

The main objectives of this analysis are:

To evaluate overall sales performance
To identify top-performing regions and products
To analyze sales trends over time
To provide actionable business insights


3. Dataset Overview

The dataset contains sales transactions including:

Product categories
Retailers
Regions
Units sold
Revenue
Profit

Tools used for analysis:

Microsoft Excel
SQL (PostgreSQL)
Power BI / Tableau
Python (optional analysis)


4. Data Cleaning & Preparation

The following data preparation steps were performed:

Removal of duplicate records
Handling of missing values
Standardization of column names
Conversion of date formats
Data validation for consistency


5. Analysis Performed

The following analyses were conducted:

Sales trend analysis over time
Regional sales comparison
Product category performance
Retailer performance analysis
Profitability analysis


6. Key Insights

The West region recorded the highest total sales.
Footwear products generated the highest revenue.
Sales peaked during Q4, indicating seasonal demand.
Certain retailers consistently outperformed others in revenue generation.
Profit margins varied significantly across product categories.


7. Recommendations

Focus marketing efforts on high-performing regions.
Increase inventory for top-selling products (especially footwear).
Improve strategies in underperforming regions.
Strengthen partnerships with top-performing retailers.
Optimize pricing strategies to improve profit margins.


8. Conclusion

The analysis provides valuable insights into Adidas sales performance. By focusing on high-performing segments and improving weaker areas, Adidas can increase revenue and profitability.
